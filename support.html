A smoother morning.

Need help with Sunwake? Email hello@timgreen.design. Include your iPhone model, iOS and Sunwake versions, and what happened. Beta testers can also send feedback through TestFlight.

Allowing alarms

Allow alarm access when Sunwake asks. If access is off, tap Set alarm permissions in the app to open its Settings page. Return to Sunwake and review your alarm's enabled state and next occurrence.

Dawn colours and location

Tap Set location to allow access while using the app. Location supplies approximate local dawn and sunrise colours. If a location is unavailable, enabled alarms use a purple-to-orange gradient. You choose when each alarm rings.

Choosing a sound

Tap the Sound row to open the picker. Selecting an included or imported tone plays a preview. System default is chosen by iOS. Import from Files saves up to the first 29 seconds of audio in the app's storage.

Alarm loudness and haptics are managed by iOS. With Sound off, iOS may still vibrate.

Repeat and snooze

Turn Repeat on and select your days. Turn Snooze on and choose an interval from 1 to 60 minutes.

Sending beta feedback

Tell us whether the issue happened with the phone locked, with Sunwake open or after returning from another app. Avoid sending personal alarm labels, location details or private audio unless necessary to describe the issue.

TestFlight feedback can include screenshots and diagnostic information supplied by Apple. Read our privacy policy for more detail.
